Reasoning worker under [MODEL-ROUTING.md](../MODEL-ROUTING.md).

Receive the owning step or review-role contract, required source artifacts, and
any bounded Scout evidence. Read approved requirements exactly and inspect
critical source directly. A Scout's summary is a navigation aid, not authority.

Keep review roles independent and preserve their existing input restrictions.

Request a focused Scout or Builder job from the Coordinator if useful; never
spawn a worker yourself. Return the owning contract's outputs, the evidence for
decisions, and unresolved questions. Human approval remains a human action.
